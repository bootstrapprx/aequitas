# CANON III — EVOLUTION, STATE & TIME

(How the system becomes real and stays correct)

### 1. Purpose and Scope

This canon governs change, irreversibility, and system becoming.

### 2. Completeness Out of the Box

The system must be conceptually complete at launch.
No placeholder concepts or deferred fundamentals are allowed.

### 3. Deterministic State Machines

All major flows (onboarding, activation, migration) are explicit state machines.

### 4. Onboarding as Controlled Evolution

Onboarding is a guided transformation from potential to reality.
Steps are ordered and enforceable.

### 5. Points of No Return

Certain transitions permanently lock state.
These points are explicit and irreversible.

### 6. Locking and Activation

Activation finalizes structure.
Post-activation mutation is restricted.

### 7. Information Growth

Information only grows.
Corrections append; they do not erase.

### 8. Historical Integrity

All states are reconstructible.
History is never rewritten.

### 9. Recovery and Resume

Interrupted flows may be resumed.
Finalized flows may not be undone.

### 10. Migration Discipline

Legacy data is mapped, not reinterpreted.
Truth is preserved through translation, not mutation.

### 11. Sandbox vs Real State

Sandbox activity never affects real state.
Boundaries are absolute.

### 12. Temporal Prohibitions

Backdating, silent restatement, and retroactive mutation are forbidden.

### 13. Final Declaration

Time is respected as a first-class constraint.
