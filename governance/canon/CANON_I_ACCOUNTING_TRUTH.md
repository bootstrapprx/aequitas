# CANON I — ACCOUNTING TRUTH & STRUCTURE

*(Foundational, Immutable)* or (What is real, immutable, and non-negotiable)

**Filename (suggested):** `CANON_I_ACCOUNTING_TRUTH.md`

## 1. Purpose and Scope
Accounting truth in Aequitas exists to describe economic reality, not operational convenience.
This canon governs all representations of financial truth.

## 2. Definition of Accounting Truth
Accounting truth consists of:
balances,
obligations,
equity positions,
and flows required to produce correct financial statements.

Truth is objective, auditable, and deterministic.

## 3. Concept Over Fact
The master chart contains concepts only.
Facts (people, banks, properties, identifiers, tax forms) are prohibited.

If an element answers “which one?”, it is not accounting truth.

## 4. Clean Kernel Principle
The master chart must be:

minimal,

stable,

and sufficient.

Accounts are added only if their absence would break financial statement correctness.

Convenience is never a justification.

## 5. Standard Master Chart of Accounts
There is exactly one standard master chart.

It is GAAP-aligned.

It is not client-derived.

It is versioned, not mutable.

## 6. Root / Branch / Leaf / Fruit Model
Accounting structure is strictly separated:

Root: immutable accounting concepts

Branch: structural organization

Leaf: posting endpoints

Fruit: analytical facts and context

No layer may absorb the role of another.

## 7. Double-Entry and Balance Invariants
Every transaction must:

debit and credit equally,

preserve balance sheet integrity,

reconcile through a trial balance.

Violations are system errors.

## 8. Normal Balance Rules
Account behavior is determined by nature:

Assets / Expenses → debit-normal

Liabilities / Equity / Revenue → credit-normal

This behavior is invariant.

## 9. Mandatory Accounts and Structural Requirements
Certain accounts must always exist to:

close periods,

compute retained earnings,

support reporting.

They cannot be removed or repurposed.

## 10. Normalization Rules (Form vs Meaning)
The system must never:

silently rename,

silently merge,

silently reinterpret user intent.

Form may be corrected; meaning must be preserved.
## 11. Immutability and Versioning of Accounting Truth
Accounting truth:

is versioned,

never overwritten,

corrected only through additive history.

## 12. Prohibited Structures and Anti-Patterns
Forbidden:

fact-encoded accounts

“other” as a dumping category

tax-driven account names

instance-level master accounts

## 13. Final Declaration of Accounting Reality
If something conflicts with this canon, it is not accounting truth.
