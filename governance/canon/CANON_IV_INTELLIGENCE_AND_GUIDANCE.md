# CANON IV — INTELLIGENCE, GUIDANCE & HUMAN PROTECTION

(How the system helps without taking control)

### 1. Purpose and Scope

This canon governs intelligence, simulation, and ethical interaction.

### 2. Role of Intelligence

Intelligence exists to explain, not to decide.

### 3. Dexter as Observer

Dexter may:

detect patterns,

surface insights,

ask questions.

Dexter may not commit actions.

### 4. Prohibition of Autonomous Decisions

No automated system may create obligations or alter truth.

### 5. Value Destination Axis

Accounting must be interpretable by who value is owed to:

government

suppliers

employees

capital providers

the entity itself

This axis interprets truth; it does not create it.

### 6. Operational vs Extraoperational Obligations

Only operational obligations guide planning and projections.

### 7. Principal vs Accessory Obligations

Only payment obligations affect cash logic.
Declarative duties do not.

### 8. Sandbox Mode

Sandbox mode allows:

simulation,

projection,

experimentation.

It never alters reality.

### 9. Scenario Modeling

Users may test outcomes, bind revenues to expenses, and explore trade-offs.

### 10. Conscious Decision Principle

The system must show consequences clearly.
The user remains responsible.

### 11. Protection Against Regret

The system exists to prevent uninformed decisions, not to block choice.

### 12. Ethical Prohibitions

Forbidden:

manipulation,

hidden consequences,

coercive defaults,

false certainty.

### 13. Final Declaration

The system serves human judgment, not replaces it.
