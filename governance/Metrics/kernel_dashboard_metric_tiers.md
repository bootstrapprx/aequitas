# PASS 1 — Kernel Dashboard Metric Tiers

Purpose of tiers:

* Reduce cognitive load
* Prevent premature interpretation
* Ensure trust before sophistication

Each tier answers **one kind of question only**.

---

## TIER 1 — CORE METRICS

**Question answered:**

> “Is the company alive and functioning right now?”

These metrics must:

* Be instantly understandable
* Never contradict each other
* Require zero accounting knowledge to interpret

### 1. Total Cash

**Why it’s Tier 1**
Cash is non-negotiable reality. No assumptions.

---

### 2. Net Revenue

**Why it’s Tier 1**
This defines whether the company is producing economic value at all.

---

### 3. Operating Income

**Why it’s Tier 1**
Separates operations from financing and tax artifacts.

---

### 4. Net Working Capital

**Why it’s Tier 1**
This shows whether operations can continue without external help.

---

### 5. Current Ratio (Kernel Approximation)

**Why it’s Tier 1**
Simple liquidity stress signal.

---

**Tier 1 Exit Condition**
If Tier 1 looks unhealthy, **nothing else is shown** except explanations.
No projections. No diagnostics. No simulations.

---

## TIER 2 — ADVANCED METRICS

**Question answered:**

> “How well is the company operating?”

These metrics:

* Explain *why* Tier 1 looks the way it does
* Introduce ratios and efficiency
* Assume the user understands basic financial relationships

---

### 6. Gross Margin

**Why it’s Tier 2**
Requires understanding of cost structure.

---

### 7. Operating Margin

**Why it’s Tier 2**
Explains scalability and operational leverage.

---

### 8. Payroll Ratio

**Why it’s Tier 2**
Introduces structural cost analysis.

---

### 9. Operating Expense Ratio

**Why it’s Tier 2**
Explains burn rate dynamics.

---

### 10. Days Sales Outstanding (DSO)

**Why it’s Tier 2**
Connects revenue to cash discipline.

---

### 11. Days Payable Outstanding (DPO)

**Why it’s Tier 2**
Introduces supplier financing dynamics.

---

### 12. Cash Conversion Cycle (Kernel Version)

**Why it’s Tier 2**
Requires understanding of operational timing.

---

**Tier 2 Exit Condition**
Tier 2 metrics are shown **only after Tier 1 stability is confirmed**.

---

## TIER 3 — DIAGNOSTIC METRICS

**Question answered:**

> “Is the accounting telling the truth?”

These metrics:

* Detect illusions
* Reveal hidden risk
* Are not meant for casual users

---

### 13. Operating Cash Flow (Estimated)

**Why it’s Tier 3**
This challenges reported profit.

---

### 14. Earnings Quality Ratio

**Why it’s Tier 3**
Explicitly measures truth vs appearance.

---

### 15. Refund Rate

**Why it’s Tier 3**
Detects quality failures in revenue.

---

### 16. Tax Coverage Ratio

**Why it’s Tier 3**
Detects silent fiscal stress.

---

### 17. Tax Drift Indicator

**Why it’s Tier 3**
Requires accrual awareness.

---

### 18. Depreciation Intensity

**Why it’s Tier 3**
Signals long-term capital decay.

---

### 19. Asset Intensity Ratio

**Why it’s Tier 3**
Distinguishes scalable vs capital-heavy models.

---

**Tier 3 Rule**
Diagnostic metrics:

* Never auto-trigger actions
* Never display alone
* Always include explanatory text

---

## Projection & Simulation Metrics (Special Tier)

These are **not metrics**.
They are **questions posed to the system**.

They are only enabled when:

* Tier 1 is stable
* Tier 2 is understood
* Tier 3 is visible but not alarming

Examples:

* Revenue growth scenarios
* Cash stress simulations
* DSO/DPO sensitivity

They live **outside** the tier hierarchy.
