# KERNEL DASHBOARD v1.0

**Status:** Frozen Specification
**Layer:** Intelligence / Interpretation
**Depends on:** Kernel L0 Accounts Only
**Audience:** Humans (founders, accountants, auditors, investors)

---

## 1. Definition of Kernel Dashboard v1.0

Kernel Dashboard v1.0 is the **first complete, stable interpretation layer** built exclusively on top of the Kernel.

It exists to:

* expose financial reality,
* explain structural relationships,
* reveal risk and illusion,
* without directing behavior or automating decisions.

It is **not**:

* a management system,
* a forecasting engine,
* an optimization tool,
* or a substitute for professional judgment.

---

## 2. Included Scope (What v1.0 Contains)

Kernel Dashboard v1.0 consists of **three inseparable components**:

1. **Metric Tier System**
2. **Canonical Interpretation Rules**
3. **Human-Centered Layout Logic**

If any of these is removed or bypassed, the dashboard is no longer Kernel-compliant.

---

## 3. Metric Tier System (Locked)

### Tier 1 — Core Metrics (Always Visible)

Purpose: survival and immediacy.

Included:

* Total Cash
* Net Revenue
* Operating Income
* Net Working Capital
* Current Ratio (kernel approximation)

Rules:

* Always first
* Always visible
* Never overridden
* No projections allowed

---

### Tier 2 — Advanced Metrics (Conditionally Visible)

Purpose: operational understanding.

Included:

* Gross Margin
* Operating Margin
* Payroll Ratio
* Operating Expense Ratio
* DSO
* DPO
* Cash Conversion Cycle (kernel version)

Rules:

* Visible only after Tier 1 stability
* Grouped by meaning (profit, cost, timing)
* Explanatory, never judgmental

---

### Tier 3 — Diagnostic Metrics (Explicit Access Only)

Purpose: truth and risk detection.

Included:

* Operating Cash Flow (estimated)
* Earnings Quality Ratio
* Refund Rate
* Tax Coverage Ratio
* Tax Drift Indicator
* Depreciation Intensity
* Asset Intensity Ratio

Rules:

* Hidden by default
* Explicitly labeled as diagnostic
* Mandatory explanations
* No alerts, no scores, no automation

---

### Simulations (Out of Tier System)

Purpose: hypothetical exploration.

Rules:

* Separate space
* User-initiated
* Assumptions explicit
* Never persisted as truth

---

## 4. Canonical Interpretation Rules (Locked)

The dashboard:

* may explain,
* may compare,
* may decompose,
* may contextualize.

The dashboard may **never**:

* decide,
* recommend,
* optimize,
* auto-correct,
* rank,
* gamify,
* or moralize data.

All metrics must be:

* derivable from Kernel L0 accounts,
* auditable by a human,
* reproducible manually.

---

## 5. Layout & Experience Logic (Locked)

### Entry Experience

* User lands on Tier 1 only
* Five cards
* Five seconds to understand reality

### Progressive Depth

* Vertical navigation only
* Depth unlocked by intent, not curiosity traps

### Tone Control

* Tier 1: neutral, calm
* Tier 2: explanatory
* Tier 3: deliberate, slow, explicit

### Separation of Real vs Hypothetical

* Actuals and simulations never mixed
* Visual, textual, and spatial separation mandatory

---

## 6. Role-Based Default Views (v1.0 Constraint)

Kernel Dashboard v1.0 supports **roles without forks**.

### Founder Default

* Tier 1 fully visible
* Tier 2 collapsed but accessible
* Tier 3 hidden

Purpose:

> “Am I safe, and why?”

---

### Accountant / CFO Default

* Tier 1 visible
* Tier 2 visible
* Tier 3 accessible with one action

Purpose:

> “Is this consistent, defensible, and explainable?”

---

### Auditor / Reviewer Default

* Tier 1 visible
* Tier 2 visible
* Tier 3 visible by default
* Simulations disabled

Purpose:

> “Is there illusion, drift, or silent risk?”

---

**Important Rule**
Roles change **visibility**, not **logic**.
No role gets different math.

---

## 7. Onboarding Alignment (v1.0)

During onboarding, the user must explicitly acknowledge:

1. The Kernel uses **few accounts by design**
2. The dashboard **explains, not decides**
3. Projections are **hypothetical**
4. No enriched chart is required for truth

Unlock order during onboarding:

1. Tier 1 explanation
2. Tier 2 explanation
3. Tier 3 warning
4. Simulation disclaimer

No skipping.

---

## 8. What Is Explicitly Deferred (Not in v1.0)

These are **intentionally excluded** from Kernel Dashboard v1.0:

* Industry benchmarks
* Peer comparisons
* AI-generated recommendations
* Automatic alerts
* Scoring systems
* Creditworthiness labels
* “Health” grades
* One-click actions

They may exist only in **non-kernel modules**, under stricter Canons.

---

## 9. Forward Compatibility Rules

Future versions may:

* add enriched analytics
* add industry overlays
* add explanatory narratives
* add education layers

They may not:

* bypass Tier order
* alter Kernel math
* mix actuals with simulations
* inject decision authority

Kernel Dashboard v1.0 is the **constitutional baseline**.

---

## 10. Definition of “Done” for v1.0

Kernel Dashboard v1.0 is considered complete when:

* All Tier 1–3 metrics exist and reconcile
* Each metric is explainable in isolation
* No action is suggested anywhere
* A founder and an auditor can sit together
  and agree on what the dashboard says

If they disagree on *what to do*,
the system has succeeded.

---

## Closing Statement

Kernel Dashboard v1.0 does not try to be smart.
It tries to be **honest**.

It assumes:

* humans are intelligent,
* reality is complex,
* and clarity beats confidence.

This is not a product flourish.
This is a **line in the sand**.
