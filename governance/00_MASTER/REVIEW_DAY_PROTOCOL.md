# Review Day Protocol (🟦)

**Mode:** Synthesis, planning, retrospection.
**Target Duration:** 2–4 hours.

> **Philosophy:** "We stop to look at the map, so we don't walk off a cliff."

---

## 1. Purpose
*   To audit the week's work against the Roadmap.
*   To identify "drift" from the Canon.
*   To plan the next week's generic schedule (Heavy/Light flow).

## 2. Checklist
- [ ] **Review Daily Notes:** Read the last 5-7 daily notes.
- [ ] **Check Divergences:** Did we deviate? Was it necessary?
- [ ] **Update Calendar:** Fill in the Weekly Table for the upcoming week.
- [ ] **Update Roadmap:** Mark items as Done or Blocked.

## 3. Output
*   A crisp decision on what the **Primary Focus** is for next week.
*   An updated **Weekly Table** in [[CALENDAR]].
