# Routines & Protocols

> **Why routines exist:**
> To protect cognition, reduce friction, and automate the start/stop cost of complex work.
> Routines are **constraints**, not productivity hacks. They narrow the decision space so you can focus on the *content* rather than the *process*.

---

## The Protocol Layer

We use explicit protocols to define the state of mind required for different types of work.

### 1. [[LIGHT_DAY_PROTOCOL]]
**For Integration & Stabilization.**
*   **Purpose:** To recover from heavy cognitive load while maintaining momentum.
*   **Key Rule:** No new Canons. No architectural decisions.
*   **Tone:** Calm, protective, non-punitive.

### 2. [[HEAVY_DAY_PROTOCOL]]
**For Deep Architecture & Canon Work.**
*   **Purpose:** To solve distinct, hard problems with full context.
*   **Key Rule:** Single focus. Mandatory disconnect.
*   **Tone:** Rigorous, intense, final.

### 3. [[REVIEW_DAY_PROTOCOL]]
**For Synthesis & Planning.**
*   **Purpose:** To zoom out, audit the week, and prepare the next cycle.
*   **Key Rule:** No execution. Only observation and planning.

---

## How Routines Interact with the Calendar

1.  **Check the Calendar ([[CALENDAR]])** first thing in the morning.
2.  **Load the correct Protocol** based on the Day Mode (🟥/🟩/🟦).
3.  **Execute the Entry Checklist** defined in that protocol.
4.  **Work** according to the constraints.
5.  **Execute the Shutdown Ritual** to close the day.

> **Note:** You cannot "try harder" to make a Light Day into a Heavy Day. If the calendar says Light, you respect the constraint. This protects your ability to do Heavy work tomorrow.
