# Light Day Protocol (🟩)

**Mode:** Stabilization, cleanup, integration.
**Target Duration:** 4–7 hours.
**Tone:** Calm, protective, non-punitive.

> **Philosophy:** "A Light Day is not a failed Heavy Day. It is how heavy systems survive."

---

## 1. Purpose
*   To recover cognitive resources after Heavy work.
*   To integrate loose ends and stabilize the vault.
*   To prepare the context for the next Heavy push.
*   To reduce "entropy" (renaming, organizing, linking).

## 2. Allowed Activities
*   **Documentation:** Updating manuals, fixing typos, adding examples.
*   **Refactoring:** Renaming notes, moving files to correct folders.
*   **Alignment Checks:** Verifying that recent work matches the Roadmap.
*   **Writing Prompts:** Improving or documenting AI prompts.
*   **Research:** Reading (but not deciding).
*   **Preparation:** Setting up the workspace for tomorrow's Heavy block.
*   **Small Fixes:** addressing minor "papercuts".

## 3. Explicit Prohibitions (🚫)
*   **NO New Canons:** Do not define new laws or truths.
*   **NO Algorithms:** Do not design complex logic or state machines.
*   **NO "Heroics":** If it feels hard, stop.
*   **NO Deadline Panic:** Light days are for quality, not speed.

## 4. Definition of Done
A Light Day is complete when **one** of the following is true:
1.  The system is visibly cleaner than it was this morning.
2.  A blocking ambiguity for tomorrow is removed.
3.  You feel "reset" and ready for the next Heavy Day.
4.  4 hours of low-intensity honest work are logged.

## 5. Closure
*   Log the day in the Daily Note.
*   Confirm tomorrow's plan in [[CALENDAR]].
*   Close Obsidian. You are done.
